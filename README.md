## 🎯 About this software

Workdaybar helps you track the time you spend on tasks throughout your day. Many people struggle to see where their time goes while they work. This tool sits on your taskbar and records your activity with minimal effort. You gain clear insights into your habits and improve your focus. The application runs quietly in the background without interfering with your work.

## 💻 System requirements

Your computer must run Windows 10 or Windows 11. You need at least 200MB of free disk space to store your logs. The software requires a stable internet connection for the installation process, though it works offline after setup. We recommend using a modern processor for the best performance.

## 📌 Using the software

When you open the application, you see a main window. Click the Start button to begin tracking your work. If you need to take a break, click the Pause button. You can stop or save your current session by clicking the Stop button. To view your history, click the Reports tab at the top of the interface. The application saves your data automatically so you do not lose information when you close the program.

## ❓ Frequently asked questions

Does the software track my personal browsing? 
No, the software only tracks the time you actively log using the start and stop buttons. It respects your privacy and does not monitor private activity without your input.

Can I move my data to a new computer?
Yes. You can export your data to a file through the Settings menu. You then import this file on your new computer using the restore option.

What happens if the computer sleeps?
The tracker pauses when your computer enters sleep mode. You can resume your session once the computer wakes up.

## 🛡 Security and privacy

Your data stays on your local machine. We do not receive your usage logs or activity data. The workdaybar application does not connect to external servers to share your work patterns. You own your data entirely. 

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

If the application fails to launch, verify that you have Windows permissions to run executable files. Check that your antivirus program does not block the application. You can add an exception for the workdaybar folder in your security settings if needed. Restarting your computer often fixes general startup issues.
